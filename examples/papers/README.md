| `minerva_2206.14858.pdf` | Aitor Lewkowycz, Anders Andreassen, David Dohan, Ethan Dyer, Henryk Michalewski, Vinay Ramasesh, Ambrose Slone, Cem Anil, Imanol Schlag, Theo Gutman-Solo, Yuhuai Wu, Behnam Neyshabur, Guy Gur-Ari, Vedant Misra. *Solving Quantitative Reasoning Problems with Language Models.* arXiv:2206.14858v2, 2022. https://arxiv.org/abs/2206.14858 | [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) |

The PDF is redistributed unchanged under its licence. The slides, figure crops and numbers in
`examples/minerva/` and `docs/img/` are derived from it (figures cropped from the PDF, charts redrawn from
its tables) and carry the same attribution. The paper's authors are not affiliated with this project.

Any other paper can be used: pass its PDF to `studio/tools/headless_run.py --pdf ...` or drop it into the
Studio. Papers under the arXiv non-exclusive licence (most arXiv papers) can be processed locally but should
not be committed to a public repository.
